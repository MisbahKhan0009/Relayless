# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Relayless to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including when disagreeing
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the people who use Relayless

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/MisbahKhan0009/Relayless/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the maintainer privately, even though the form is meant for security reports) or by contacting the maintainer via their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
